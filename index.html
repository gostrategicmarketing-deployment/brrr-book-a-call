The system

Five steps. In this order.

The BRRR method Cindy teaches, step by step. On the call we look at where you'd start.

01

Buy

Find a house that needs work and run it through the rule Cindy uses and the comps checklist, with an asset-based lender who cares about the deal, not your paycheck.

02

Renovate

Real numbers from a contractor, line by line. A Scope of Work Template and a Rehab Budget Calculator keep the budget honest.

03

Rent

The Rules of Renting: rent is 30% more than your mortgage payment, Section 8 vs. private market, and qualify the tenant.

04

Refinance

Once the house is fixed and rented, refinance on its after repair value to pay back the first lender.

05

Repeat

Run the same steps on the next deal. One system, repeatable.

The rule Cindy uses

The 70% rule

What you pay for the house plus what you spend renovating it should not be more than 70% of what it's worth after the repairs.

After repair value comes from comps: same bed / same bath, similar property & square footage, similar finishes and condition, sold within the last 6 months.

Questions

Before you apply

How much money do I need to start?

Cindy's free class is built around starting with around $15K. The application asks about anything from $3,000 up. If you have under $3,000 of capital or available credit right now, please don't book a call yet: start with the free Thursday class.

Do I need 20% down?

The program is a system for buying rentals without 20% down, using asset-based lenders who qualify the property, not your paycheck.

Terms and minimums vary by lender. Lenders still review credit and set their own terms.

I've never bought a property. Is this for me?

Yes, if you're ready to do the work. In Cindy's words: "You don't need experience before you start. You need guidance."

Do I have to buy where I live?

No. Cindy started investing in North Carolina from out of state. Choosing a market is part of the program's Find The Deal step.

What does the program cost?

We cover the program and the investment on the call, once we know whether it makes sense for you.
